- phase32 formalizes staged execution outside the iCloud-backed authoritative root.
- full CTest and wrapper-heavy verification are executed from staged build roots under /tmp.
- publication occurs only after staging is complete, by copying/publishing manifests and bundle contents back into the authoritative artifact root.
- build_evidence_bundle.py now supports published snapshot mode as the official path:
  - --use-published-snapshot
  - --published-root
- publication metadata now records:
  - staging_root
  - published_root
  - publication_snapshot_id
  - source_report
  - source_manifests
  - source_manifest_hashes

- zip creation is staged in /tmp and only moved into the iCloud root at the end, avoiding long direct writes into the synced tree.
