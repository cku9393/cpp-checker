2. Policy Lifecycle Pipeline Design
- `tests/tools/run_policy_pipeline.py` now provides the operator-facing lifecycle runner with modes:
  `quick`
  `nightly`
  `full_local`
  `bundle_only`
- The runner keeps baseline/current/refresh/rerun-plan synchronized, emits machine-readable `policy_pipeline_<mode>_phase22.{json,txt}`, and attaches `recommended_next_action`.
- `quick` materializes or reuses current/refresh/rerun-plan and reports stale/reclassify state without rerunning expensive compare evidence.
- `nightly` runs `policy_nightly_refresh`, records stage timing, and then calls `build_evidence_bundle.py`.
- `bundle_only` packages already-materialized artifacts without refreshing policy evidence.

3. Severity / Exit-Code Model
- Severity is fixed as:
  `OK`
  `WARN`
  `ACTION_REQUIRED`
  `FAIL`
- Exit codes are fixed as:
  `0=OK`
  `10=WARN`
  `20=ACTION_REQUIRED`
  `30=FAIL`
- The Python runner now matches the C++ policy helper semantics:
  `OK` for `PASS/FRESH` with an empty rerun plan,
  `WARN` for stale-but-rerunnable evidence,
  `ACTION_REQUIRED` for `requires_rerun` or `reclassify_required`,
  `FAIL` for policy-gate failure or manifest/lifecycle corruption.
- `recommended_next_action` is now stable:
  `no action required; exact_shadow lifecycle remains healthy`
  `run policy_nightly_refresh or execute the rerun plan to refresh stale evidence`
  `reclassification required; rerun affected families and review exact_shadow policy retention`
  `policy gate failed; inspect failing families before approving exact_shadow`
